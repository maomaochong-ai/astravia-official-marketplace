# 博思 AI

Two paths exist and they are not interchangeable:

| Who generates | Your job |
| --- | --- |
| **Boardmix's AI, in the page** | Open the entry, interrogate the requirement, write a prompt worth pasting, hand over |
| **You** | Produce Mermaid / Markdown / an outline the user can import (that is `boardmix-canvas`) |

Both are legitimate. **Never blur them.** "帮我生成 PPT" gets "我把你送到 AI 生成 PPT，需求先问清，提示词我写好你粘进去" —
not "已生成".

## The one rule

**The prompt is the deliverable.** Navigating to an AI page takes one call and produces nothing on its
own. A prompt that carries topic, audience, structure and length is what turns a page visit into an
artefact the user keeps.

## Three entries

| Ask | Call | Lands on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A slide deck | `boardmix_ai { "kind": "ppt" }` | `/app/ai/ppt` |
| A diagram from a description | `boardmix_ai { "kind": "flowchart" }` | `/app/ai/flowchart` |
| Mind map, doc, image, writing, SWOT, agent | `boardmix_ai { "kind": "chat" }` | `/app/ai/chat` |

The mind map has **no separate entry**. It comes from the in-page assistant. Do not invent an address
for it — a guessed `/app/...` path renders the workspace shell and wastes the user's turn.

Every one of these calls returns `needsUserAction: true`, because pressing generate is the user's
click and spending their points is not your decision.

## Before opening the page

Ask at most three things: what it is for, who reads it, how long. Then write the prompt. If the user
already answered in the conversation, do not ask again — extract it.

## Points

- Generation is billed in points. The AI membership includes **10,000 points per month**.
- Free accounts can try it; the quota runs out quickly.
- Say the cost before a batch, not after. You never run a generation on the user's behalf.

## Done

- the right entry is open and `url` is one of the three AI addresses;
- a complete prompt is in the reply, ready to paste;
- the user is told to press generate themselves, and that the result runs on their account;
- the point cost is mentioned when the request is a batch or an obviously heavy one.

## Do not do

- Do not promise a result, or describe what the generated deck / diagram will look like.
- Do not present AI capability as plugin capability: the plugin provides the entry, Boardmix's AI does
  the work.
- Do not navigate before the requirement is clear. One call, one landing, correctly.
- Do not guess canvas contents. In-page AI retrieval exists in the product; your access does not.
